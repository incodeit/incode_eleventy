Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about Incode, our services and how to reach us.

What does Incode do? +

Incode is an Italian creative studio that specialises in full-stack development. We build websites, e-commerce platforms and custom digital products, and we provide UX/UI design, evaluation and consulting.

Where is Incode based? +

Incode Srl is based in Italy. The registered office is in Asola (Mantua) and the operating office is in Cremona at Co-Box, Via dell'innovazione digitale 3.

Which technologies does Incode work with? +

For websites and e-commerce we work with WordPress, WooCommerce, Drupal and Shopify. For custom projects we use Ruby on Rails, Node.js and Vue.js, plus software architecture and system integration.

How can I contact Incode? +

You can email info@incode.it or call +39 0372 027375. We work with clients as partners, not just as vendors.

When was Incode founded? +

Incode was founded in 2007 and has been building digital products for agencies and brands for nearly two decades.

How to start a project with Incode

Simple steps to begin a website, e-commerce or custom software project with us.

  1. Share your idea

    Email info@incode.it with your product idea, goals and timeline.

  2. Align on scope

    We review requirements together and define UX, technology stack and delivery plan.

  3. Build and iterate

    We design, develop and ship websites, e-commerce or custom full-stack products with you as a partner.
